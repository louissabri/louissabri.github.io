3D Printed Table

A modern, parametric table design 3D printed with recycled plastics.

Project Overview

This project explores the potential of 3D printing with recycled materials for furniture design. The table features a parametrically designed structure that optimizes material usage while maintaining structural integrity. By leveraging computational design tools and digital fabrication techniques, this project demonstrates how sustainable design can be achieved without sacrificing aesthetics or functionality.

Technologies Used

  • Grasshopper for Rhino (Parametric Modeling)
  • Karamba3D (Structural Analysis)
  • Large-format 3D printing with recycled PETG
  • Custom material testing and simulation

Design Process

The design began with a series of parametric explorations in Grasshopper, focusing on optimizing the structure based on load-bearing requirements. I developed custom algorithms to generate lattice structures that would minimize material usage while maintaining the necessary strength.

Using Karamba3D, I conducted structural analyses to refine the design and identify areas that needed reinforcement. This iterative process allowed me to achieve a balance between material efficiency and structural performance.

Fabrication

The table was fabricated using a large-format 3D printer with a custom extruder designed for recycled PETG plastic. I developed a specific printing strategy to ensure proper layer adhesion and minimize warping during the printing process.

The printing process took approximately 20 hours, with careful monitoring to ensure quality throughout. After printing, the parts were assembled and finished with an eco-friendly sealant to protect the surface.

Results & Impact

The resulting table demonstrates the viability of using recycled materials for high-quality furniture production. By using parametric design and digital fabrication, I was able to create a piece that is both sustainable and visually appealing.

This project was exhibited at the annual design showcase and received positive feedback for its innovative approach to sustainable furniture design. It has also sparked conversations about how computational design can contribute to more environmentally responsible manufacturing practices.

Future Developments

Building on this project, I am exploring ways to further optimize the printing process and expand the range of recycled materials that can be used. I am also developing an open-source version of the parametric design tools to make sustainable furniture design more accessible to other designers.
